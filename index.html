Starting My Engineering Journey

Hello! I'm Jayant Sen, a first-year undergraduate pursuing my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering (B.Tech CSE) at JECRC University, based in Rajasthan, India.

My passion for computing began with a simple question: how do lines of text turn into software that solves human problems? Stepping into university, I decided to build strong fundamentals from the ground up—starting with low-level logic in C, building clean interfaces with HTML, CSS & JavaScript, and actively experimenting with Artificial Intelligence and Prompt Engineering.

Rather than waiting for later college years, I believe in learning by creating. Whether designing utility tools for campus life or sharpening my algorithmic thinking, I treat every project as an opportunity to become a more resilient problem solver.
